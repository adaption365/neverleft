Before you dive in

Welcome in.
This one’s already yours.

No sign-up, no account. NeverLeft lives in your browser, on this device. So:

  • It’s privateOnly you can see this. Not other people, not us.
  • It’s yours to changeWe’ve set you up with a starting kit and a sample trip. Edit, rename or delete anything. You can’t break it, and nothing is shared.
  • It saves itself hereTo keep it safe or move it to your phone, you’ll make a backup later.
How is this private?

NeverLeft runs entirely in your browser. There’s no account and no server holding your things. Your kit and trips live on this device only. That makes it completely private, it works offline, and it’s instant. The trade-off: your data is tied to this browser, so if you clear it or switch devices it won’t follow you on its own. That’s what a backup is for. A small file you save or send to yourself, to restore anywhere.

What do the types mean?

Always packed · Lives with your gear, comes on most trips.

Bring from home · Grabbed from the house each trip, not stored with your gear.

Consumable · Gets used up. Scales by nights and people, flags when low.

Seasonal · Only for some conditions, like a winter sleeping bag.

Want list · Not owned yet, kept out of packing lists.
